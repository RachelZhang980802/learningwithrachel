## Important content note for the Congress chart

- The chart correctly shows Congress divided into Senate and House.
- Senate: 100 members, two per state; the Vice President is President of the Senate and can break a tied vote.
- House: 435 voting representatives apportioned by state population.
- Correct the chart verbally when teaching: Speaker of the House and Majority Leader are different roles. The vertical lines describe offices and party leadership, not a strict chain of command; Senate and House are coequal chambers.
